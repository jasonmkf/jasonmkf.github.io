Indonesia Calendar

Indonesian holidays, cuti bersama and Javanese calendar. Android.

Indonesia Calendar (Kalender Indonesia) shows national holidays and cuti bersama, school holidays by province, long weekends, and the Javanese, Hijri and Chinese lunar calendars.

Features

  • National holidays, cuti bersama and provincial anniversaries
  • School holidays, filtered by province
  • Long weekend planner
  • Javanese date and pasaran
  • Hijri date, a yearly Takwim table and Islamic dates
  • Chinese lunar calendar, almanac and zodiac outlook
  • Your own calendar events on the matching days, if you allow it
  • A home screen widget showing today

Download

Google Play

Languages

Bahasa Indonesia, English and Simplified Chinese.

Privacy

No account and no sign-up. The holiday data is built into the app, and your settings stay on your phone. Calendar access is optional. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.

Not a government app. Holiday dates can be changed by the authorities after the app is updated, so check official announcements for anything important.
