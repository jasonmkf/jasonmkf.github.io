Shelfbell

Expiry date reminders. Android and iPhone.

Shelfbell keeps track of expiry dates for food, medicine and anything else on your shelf, and reminds you before they run out. Add items by hand, by scanning a barcode, or by photographing the label; the label is read on your phone.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Shelfbell keeps a copy of your items, photos and settings in a hidden folder in your own Drive, so they stay in step across your phones and can be restored. The app asks only for access to that folder (drive.appdata). It cannot see your other files, and we run no server of our own.

Languages

English, Bahasa Melayu and Simplified Chinese.
