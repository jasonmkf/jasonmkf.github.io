Privacy Policy — Shelfbell

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Shelfbell ("the app") is made by KF Production. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your items stay on your device, and we do not run any servers that receive them.

What stays on your device

Items, dates, notes, categories, reminder settings and photos you add are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see them. The camera is used to take photos and to read barcodes and dates on labels; this reading happens on the device.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about ad interactions and app performance, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On iPhone the app does not ask to track you, so ads are not personalised. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Optional product lookup

If you turn on online product lookup, the barcode you scan is sent to Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free public product database, to find the product's name and brand. Only the barcode number is sent. Lookup is off until you allow it, and you can turn it off in Settings. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

Backup files

When you export a backup, the app creates a file with your items and photos and hands it to the place you choose (for example Files, Google Drive or email). Where it goes is your choice; the app does not upload it anywhere by itself. You can protect the file with a password.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive in Settings, the app keeps its data in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive account: your items and photos when you turn on sync, backup files when you back up by hand, and the app's settings (language, theme, reminder defaults) when you turn on Sync settings. The name you enter under Your name is saved with the items you add or change, so others using the same Google account can see who did it. The app asks Google only for access to that folder (the drive.appdata permission); it cannot see your other Drive files. The data goes straight from your device to your Google account. We run no server and cannot read it. Google's privacy policy applies to your Drive: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can disconnect at any time in Settings > Google Drive, and "Delete cloud data" there removes everything the app stored in your Drive. Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps also shows the folder's size and can delete it.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy

Android's own device backup may copy the app's item list (not the photos) to your Google account, if you have device backup turned on. This is handled by Android, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything on the device. If you connected Google Drive, use "Delete cloud data" in Settings > Google Drive to remove the copy in your Drive. We hold no copy to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
