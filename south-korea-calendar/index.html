South Korea Calendar

Korean holidays, substitute holidays and lunar calendar. Android.

South Korea Calendar (한국 달력) shows public holidays and substitute holidays, long weekends and school holidays, with Korean and Chinese lunar dates on every day.

Features

  • Public holidays and substitute holidays (대체공휴일)
  • Long weekend planner
  • School holiday dates
  • Korean and Chinese lunar dates on every day, with ganzhi and zodiac
  • Korean almanac (만세력) and Chinese almanac pages
  • Zodiac year outlook
  • Your own calendar events on the matching days, if you allow it
  • A home screen widget showing today

Download

Google Play

Languages

Korean, English and Simplified Chinese.

Privacy

No account and no sign-up. The holiday data is built into the app, and your settings stay on your phone. Calendar access is optional. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.

Not a government app. Holiday dates can be changed by the authorities after the app is updated, so check official announcements for anything important.
