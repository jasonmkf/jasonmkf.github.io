Taiwan Calendar

Taiwan holidays, make-up workdays and lunar calendar. Android.

Taiwan Calendar (台灣月曆) shows national holidays with make-up workdays, long weekends and school breaks, with the lunar calendar and the ROC (民國) year.

Features

  • National holidays and make-up workdays
  • Long weekend planner
  • School breaks
  • ROC (民國) year next to the Western year
  • Lunar calendar in Traditional Chinese
  • Farmer's almanac (農民曆)
  • Zodiac year outlook
  • Your own calendar events on the matching days, if you allow it
  • A home screen widget showing today

Download

Google Play

Languages

Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English.

Privacy

No account and no sign-up. The holiday data is built into the app, and your settings stay on your phone. Calendar access is optional. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.

Not a government app. Holiday dates can be changed by the authorities after the app is updated, so check official announcements for anything important.
