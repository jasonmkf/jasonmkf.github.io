Thailand Calendar

Thai holidays and the Thai lunar calendar. Android.

Thailand Calendar (ปฏิทินประเทศไทย) shows Thai public holidays, long weekends, and the Thai lunar calendar with the Buddhist Era year, alongside Chinese lunar and Hijri dates.

Features

  • Thai public holidays
  • Long weekend planner
  • Thai lunar calendar with the Buddhist Era year, and a Thai almanac (โหร) page
  • Chinese lunar dates and a Tong Shing almanac
  • Hijri date in the month view
  • Zodiac year outlook
  • Your own calendar events on the matching days, if you allow it
  • A home screen widget showing today

Download

Google Play

Languages

Thai, English, Bahasa Melayu, Burmese and Simplified Chinese.

Privacy

No account and no sign-up. The holiday data is built into the app, and your settings stay on your phone. Calendar access is optional. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.

Not a government app. Holiday dates can be changed by the authorities after the app is updated, so check official announcements for anything important.
